~/about.md


      

        

          
I build products where AI does the real work.

          
I'm a founding engineer at Mixar, an AI-native 3D editor. I helped take it from first commit to more than 10,000 users, and along the way I've worked on every layer: the desktop app, the backend and cloud, the AI agent at its core, and the evaluation systems that tell us whether it's getting better.

          
I adopt early by instinct. When a new model or tool comes out, I've usually already tried it. AI isn't a side tool for me but my default way of working. Agents write, test and review code alongside me every day, and I design the workflows that let them do that safely.

          
I believe the next generation of software will be built by people who know how to direct AI well. That's the craft I'm working on, and it's the future I want to help shape.
